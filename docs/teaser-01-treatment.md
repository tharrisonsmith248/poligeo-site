# PoliGeo — Teaser 01 treatment

**"The office is about to be vacant."** · 0:38 master · 3840×2160 · 23.976
Music: Liszt, *Les Préludes* — final climax (Allegro marziale animato)
Targets: Steam page · r/PoliGeo · YouTube pre-roll · social cutdowns

## The pitch

The teaser is an inauguration told by the machine that will judge it. It opens
in silence — one wire headline typing itself onto black, an empty Resolute
Desk — then Liszt's brass slams in and the engine wakes: the map, the
Congress, the Kremlin channel, a war time-lapse, the consequence lever. Cuts
land on the music's hits and accelerate with it, ending on a half-second of
dead air — *"History is in session."* — before the final chords stamp the
logo and date.

Three rules keep it coherent:

1. **Everything is diegetic.** Every frame is either game UI or the game's own
   "simulated coverage," so the teaser never promises footage the game doesn't
   contain.
2. **Data is the spectacle.** Counters rolling, tiles flipping, whip counts
   ticking — the numbers are the special effects.
3. **The player is never shown.** Only the vacant chair, a hovering hand, and
   the world reacting.

## Music map

The cut uses the closing *Allegro marziale animato* — the transfigured main
theme in full brass over a dotted march. Phrases arrive in ~2-second units,
giving eight hit points (H1–H8), a built-in caesura for the pre-title beat of
silence, and a cadence of massive final chords to land the logo. Conform exact
timecodes to the chosen recording; the proportions hold.

## Production notes

- **Music rights & conforming.** The composition is public domain; the
  recording is not. Use a PD/CC recording (Musopen has Les Préludes) or
  commission a 40-second orchestral mock-up of the marziale for exact control
  of tempo and the caesura. Conform the H1–H8 hit map to the chosen recording
  before animation starts; every cut in Acts II–III is slaved to it.
- **Editorial register.** The Les Préludes fanfare carries newsreel history —
  it was mid-century war-bulletin music. Keeping all footage in modern
  broadcast grammar (fictional outlets, clean lower-thirds, no archival-style
  film) keeps the reference at "the news is scoring your presidency," which is
  the game's own fiction, and nothing else. Real leaders appear only as the
  game's UI depicts them; AI reels use generic anchors and crowds — no real
  persons, no real network branding.
- **Type & grade.** All on-screen text in the site's system: Marcellus for
  cards, ivory on ink, gold reserved for the single imperative word per card
  (VACANT, VOTES, PRICED, WAR, ANSWERS, WATCHING, UNBOUNDED, SESSION). Grade
  everything toward the site palette so UI captures and AI reels sit in one
  world.
- **Sound design.** A thin diegetic layer mapped to cuts: ticker keys, gavel
  crack, message key-click, sonar pings, stamp thud — then nothing in the
  caesura. The silence at 0:29.5 only lands if the bed underneath was busy.
- **Deliverables.** Master 0:38 · 3840×2160 · 23.976. Cutdowns: 0:30 (drop
  0:22–0:26, pre-roll), 0:15 (Acts I + IV plus H1/H4/H5, socials), 9:16 and
  1:1 crops for Reddit/Shorts. Poster frame at 0:33 for the Steam page.
  Loop-friendly: last frame matches first.
