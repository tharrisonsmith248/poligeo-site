# PoliGeo — Teaser 01 treatment (v2)

**"The office is about to be vacant."**
0:38 Steam cut · 0:42 YouTube cut · label: **Announcement Teaser**
Music: Liszt, *Les Préludes* — final climax (Allegro marziale animato)
Date card: **COMING TO STEAM · OCTOBER 2026** · CTA: **Wishlist now on Steam**

v2 restructures the teaser around the conventions of Paradox announcement
teasers (CK3 *An Heir is Born*, Victoria 3, EU5, HoI4, Stellaris) and Valve's
Steam trailer guidance.

## What Paradox teasers do, and how we apply it

| Convention | Confirmed in | Teaser 01 |
|---|---|---|
| One governing conceit, never a feature montage | CK3 snake-in-the-cradle; Vic3 animated illustrations + litany VO; EU5 golden paintbrush; HoI4 Churchill speech; Stellaris space vistas | The vacant office and the machine that will judge whoever takes it; text cards join into one sentence |
| Literary register (VO essay/litany, or text cards + music) | Vic3 "The railroad and the telegraph…"; Stellaris text-cards-only | Text cards (music leaves no room for VO): *One engine. Every vote. Every word. Every war. Every consequence. Computed in real time.* |
| Title card at ~80–90% of runtime, after a pre-title sting | CK3 cuts to logo on the snake cliffhanger | Title at 0:30 of 0:38 (79%), right after the caesura sting |
| Pre-title sting, **no post-logo stinger** | None of the five has a post-logo stinger | The hand that hovers over the lever and doesn't pull; nothing after the end slate |
| Date card is a window, never an exact date | CK3 "2020"; Imperator "early 2019"; Vic3/EU5/Stellaris no date at announce | "October 2026" month-window; exact day saved for a Release Date trailer |
| End slate: logo + platform + wishlist CTA, held ≥4 s | Vic3 "Wishlist now on Steam"; EU5 "Wishlist EU5 Today"; craft guidance: CTA ~4 s | Slate holds 0:34–0:38 with wordmark, Steam mark, date, boxed CTA, poligeo.org · r/PoliGeo |
| Footage disclaimer | Paradox stamps CGI "Not actual gameplay"; in-engine work gets "pre-alpha footage" | "PRE-ALPHA ENGINE FOOTAGE · SIMULATED BROADCAST IMAGERY", small type in the opening seconds |
| No rating slate on PC/Steam announce cuts | No Paradox announce teaser opens with an ESRB slate; Steam doesn't require ratings | No slate; add ESRB RP only for console/TV placements |
| Logo sting on YouTube; content-first on Steam | Modern Paradox teasers open on the publisher wordmark; Valve: show the game in ~5 s, first trailer autoplays muted | Two cuts: YouTube +4 s PG-cube sting; Steam cut opens on content at frame one, every beat carded for sound-off viewing |
| Honest label + teaser → gameplay-trailer cadence | Paradox always follows announce with a separate gameplay trailer | "Announcement Teaser" now; "Gameplay Trailer" (narrated live demos) is the next beat |

**One deliberate break:** Paradox withholds gameplay at announce because their
teasers are outsourced CGI. PoliGeo's differentiator is a real engine that
already runs — and Valve prefers content over cinematics — so this teaser
shows true engine/UI footage shot cinematically. That is the credibility play
against Power & Revolution's pre-rendered promises.

## Music map — how the cut sits on the melody

The closing *Allegro marziale animato*: the work's lyrical second theme
transfigured into a C-major brass march over a dotted rhythm, built from
square phrases — which is what makes it cuttable.

| Region | Music | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Silence → timpani roll (the bar before the marziale) swelling to the downbeat | Cold open |
| 0:06–0:14 | Theme, first statement: antecedent (H1–H2) + consequent (H3–H4), one shot per 2 s half-phrase | Litany, clauses 1–4 |
| 0:14–0:22 | Theme, second statement, fuller orchestration: H5 gets the 4 s broadening phrase, H6 + one beat ride the answer | Litany, clauses 5–6 |
| 0:22–0:29 | Cadential drive (stretto): germ motif hammered in sequence; cutting doubles to ~1 s per shot | No guardrails |
| 0:29–0:30 | Caesura — engineered half-second of dead silence | "History is in session." |
| 0:30–0:38 | Three cadence chords: title / tagline / slate; chord 3 held with timpani; cut to black on the release | Title + end slate |

Conforming: the composition is public domain, the recording is not. Source a
PD/CC recording (Musopen) or commission a 40-second mock-up of the marziale
(buys exact tempo, a written-in caesura, chords timed to the slate). Conform
the H1–H8 map to the recording before animation starts.

## Beat sheet, second by second (Steam cut, 0:38)

YouTube cut prepends a 4 s PG-cube sting on black; all timecodes +4 s.
Sources: [SITE] poligeo.org interactive re-rendered at 4K · [ENGINE] running
game · [AI] generated, graded into "simulated coverage" · [BRAND] logo assets.

### Act I — Cold open (0:00–0:06), before the music

- **0:00 (1.5 s)** — Content at frame one, no logos. A gold mono cursor
  blinks, then one Wire line types out: *"White House weighs a new sanctions
  package on Tehran."* Bottom-left small type: PRE-ALPHA ENGINE FOOTAGE ·
  SIMULATED BROADCAST IMAGERY. Sound: ticker keys, distant newsroom. [SITE]
- **0:01.5 (2.5 s)** — Three news-reel insets (~0.8 s each), boxed 4:3,
  scanlines, SIMULATED COVERAGE watermark: anchor desk, night motorcade,
  carrier deck at dawn. Fictional-outlet lower-thirds. Timpani roll fades in.
  [AI + SITE]
- **0:04 (2.0 s)** — Slow push-in on the empty Resolute Desk, chair turned
  away (AI still + 2.5D parallax — the Victoria 3 animated-illustration
  technique). Card: **"The most powerful office on Earth is about to be
  VACANT."** Timpani swell peaks into the downbeat. [AI]

### Act II — The litany (0:06–0:22), one clause per downbeat

Victoria 3's litany figure, done in text cards; across seven shots the cards
assemble one sentence, so muted Steam autoplay still reads the whole pitch.

- **0:06 · H1 (2 s)** — *Cymbal crash; theme, antecedent bar 1.* Pull-back
  from Washington to the globe; 4,454 provinces flood-fill; counter races
  199 NATIONS · 14,262 TILES. Card: **"ONE ENGINE."** [ENGINE]
- **0:08 · H2 (2 s)** — *Antecedent bar 3.* The 119th Congress hemicycle
  snaps in caucus by caucus; Freedom Caucus pulses gold; whip counter grinds
  214… 217… 218; gavel crack. Card: **"EVERY VOTE."** [SITE]
- **0:10 · H3 (2 s)** — *Consequent begins, a step higher.* Kremlin secure
  channel; Putin's line slides in — *"Nothing comes off before you end this
  war."* Three priced replies flick past; one locks. Card: **"EVERY WORD."**
  [SITE]
- **0:12 · H4 (2 s)** — *Consequent cadence, cymbal on the resolution.*
  Southern Shield time-lapse: 270 tiles flood blue week 1 → 10; stat column
  spins (casualties, refugees, $B). Card: **"EVERY WAR."** [SITE/ENGINE]
- **0:14 · H5 (4 s)** — *Second statement, the broadening phrase; card lands
  in two parts on its two downbeats.* Hand pulls "Blockade the Strait of
  Hormuz"; split screen erupts — oil spikes, approval dives, markets cascade,
  The Wire floods. Card: **"EVERY CONSEQUENCE."** [SITE + AI hand]
- **0:18 · H6 (2 s)** — *Answering phrase.* Ukraine war room seeded to the
  real 20 Jan 2025 front; counters roll 430,000 / 700,000; stamp slams:
  **INTENSITY: EXISTENTIAL** (no card — the stamp is the card). [SITE]
- **0:20 (2 s)** — *Second statement closes into the drive.* PAM: 203 agent
  dots swing, project to states, barometer shivers at 269–269 and tips;
  half-second insets of confetti/protest. Card: **"COMPUTED IN REAL TIME."**
  [SITE + AI crowds]

### Act III — No guardrails (0:22–0:30), the stretto

- **0:22 · H7 (1 s)** — *Drive begins; cuts drop to ~1 s.* One anchor becomes
  a mosaic of twelve emergency broadcasts in five languages. [AI + SITE]
- **0:23 (1 s)** — A cable types itself — *"a captured president simply goes
  missing"* — redaction bars strike through the words live. [AI gfx]
- **0:24 (2×1 s)** — A province flips crimson tile by tile; missile arcs
  cross the map, intercept percentages count down; sonar pings. [ENGINE]
- **0:26 · H8 (2 s)** — *Peak tutti.* Nuclear posture ladder steps 4 → 3 → 2,
  one step per brass hit; cold data, no mushroom clouds. Card: **"POWER IS
  UNBOUNDED."** [ENGINE]
- **0:28 (1.5 s)** — *Last drive bar cuts off.* **Pre-title sting** (the CK3
  snake move): the hand returns to the lever, hovers, and does **not** pull.
  [AI + SITE]
- **0:29.5 (0.5 s)** — *Caesura, dead silence.* Black; one line types
  instantly: **"History is in session."** [SITE copy]

### Act IV — Title + end slate (0:30–0:38), on the final chords

- **0:30 · chord 1 (2 s)** — **Title card at 79% of runtime**: map tiles from
  every prior shot lock into the PG cube; the map-fill POLIGEO wordmark
  resolves beneath. [BRAND]
- **0:32 · chord 2 (2 s)** — Tagline, one line, the EU5 "Be Ambitious" slot:
  *"The ultimate geopolitical simulator, running in real time."* [BRAND]
- **0:34 · chord 3, held (4 s)** — **End slate, held four full seconds**:
  POLIGEO wordmark · **COMING TO STEAM · OCTOBER 2026** · Steam logo ·
  boxed **WISHLIST NOW ON STEAM** · poligeo.org · r/PoliGeo. No post-logo
  stinger; the only living element is the Wire cursor blinking in the corner
  (seamless loop to frame one). Cut to black exactly on the chord release.

## Production notes

- **Cuts & specs.** Master: 0:42 YouTube cut, 3840×2160, 60 fps (UI reads
  crisp at 60), H.264/AAC. Steam cut: 0:38, no logo sting, content at frame
  one; Steam delivery caps at 1080p — encode 1080p60, 5,000+ Kbps; first
  trailer autoplays muted, so every beat is carded; upload captions. Socials:
  0:15 vertical re-stack (Act I + H1/H5 + title/slate) in 9:16 and 1:1.
  Label: "PoliGeo — Announcement Teaser." Poster frame at 0:34. Steam allows
  two trailers before screenshots — slot 2 reserved for the Gameplay Trailer.
- **Music rights & conforming.** Composition PD; recording is not. PD/CC
  recording (Musopen) or a commissioned 40 s marziale mock-up. Conform the
  H1–H8 hit map before animation starts.
- **Editorial register.** The Les Préludes fanfare carries newsreel history —
  mid-century war-bulletin music. Modern broadcast grammar only (fictional
  outlets, clean lower-thirds, no archival-style film) keeps the reference at
  "the news is scoring your presidency." Real leaders appear only as the
  game's UI depicts them; AI reels use generic anchors and crowds.
- **Standard-elements checklist.** Title card ✓ (0:30) · tagline ✓ (0:32) ·
  date card ✓ (Coming to Steam · October 2026) · platform logo ✓ (Steam) ·
  wishlist CTA ✓ (boxed, held 4 s) · site/community line ✓ · footage
  disclaimer ✓ (opening seconds) · rating slate ✗ (PC/Steam cuts don't use
  one) · post-logo stinger ✗ (Paradox convention: sting before the title).
- **Sound design.** Diegetic layer mapped to cuts: ticker keys, gavel crack,
  key-click, sonar pings, stamp thud — then nothing in the caesura. The
  silence at 0:29.5 only lands if the bed underneath was busy.
