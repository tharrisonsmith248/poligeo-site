# PoliGeo — Teaser 02 storyline ("One Term")

The narrative cut: one presidency's arc, from a landslide to martial law,
opened and closed by the game's own title. 0:57 · Les Préludes climax ·
**COMING TO STEAM · OCTOBER 2026** · Wishlist now on Steam.

Supersedes the beat order in `teaser-01-treatment.md` (whose conventions —
title at ~80%, pre-title sting, month-window date card, wishlist end slate,
content-first Steam cut — all still apply). Styleframes for every beat are in
`../trailer/frames/`; fal clip specs in `../trailer/FAL-NOTES.md`.

## The arc

Title intro — **PoliGeo · The Ultimate Geopolitical Simulator** (the game states itself first, so nothing that follows reads as a political ad).
Act I — **The mandate** (win in a landslide; the world is watching).
Act II — **The world you inherit** (Ukraine 2022→2025; talk to the Kremlin, choose hostility; order the Minsk snatch).
Act III — **Southern Shield** (invade Venezuela; install a regime; inherit an insurgency).
Act IV — **Iran** (covert unrest + air campaign; decapitation strikes; the regime's crackdown; Hormuz closes).
Act V — **The home front** (unrest at home; order the crackdown; defy the courts; martial law).
Title — the bookend: logo, *History is in session.*, date and wishlist.

## Notes

- **Cuts:** one master 0:57 for Steam and YouTube — the title intro doubles as the logo sting, so no separate cuts are needed.
  Cutdowns: 0:30 (Acts I, II-compressed, title), 0:15 (election → one war
  beat → title) for socials, 9:16/1:1 crops.
- **Register:** everything is the game's own fiction — in-game UI, simulated
  broadcast, fictional outlets. Real 2024 election numbers are public record;
  every hypothetical (Belarus, Venezuela, Iran, martial law) is framed by
  in-game UI — and the film opens on the game's own title — so it reads as
  *simulation*, never as news or a political ad. The disclaimer runs
  in the opening seconds. No real faces in generated footage; real leaders
  appear only as the game's UI depicts them.
- **Music:** composition PD, recording is not (Musopen or commissioned
  mock-up); conform the hit map first. The marziale's two statements carry
  Acts II–III, the stretto carries Iran and the home front, the caesura and
  three chords carry the sting, line and slate.
