## The arc

Act I — **The mandate** (win in a landslide; the machine is watching).
Act II — **The world you inherit** (Ukraine 2022→2025; talk to the Kremlin, choose hostility; order the Minsk snatch).
Act III — **Southern Shield** (invade Venezuela; install a regime; inherit an insurgency).
Act IV — **Iran** (covert unrest + air campaign; decapitation strikes; the regime's crackdown; Hormuz closes).
Act V — **The home front** (unrest at home; order the crackdown; defy the courts; martial law).
Title — *The Ultimate Geopolitical Simulator.*

  appear only as the game's UI depicts them.

  mock-up); conform the hit map first. The marziale's two statements carry
  Acts II–III, the stretto carries Iran and the home front, the caesura and
