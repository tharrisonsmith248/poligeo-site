From the development desk · August 2026

Every one of PoliGeo's 199 nations runs its own economy, and none of them pauses for you. One deterministic engine ticks forward in real time: output, prices, currencies, markets and jobs are recomputed continuously, whether you are watching or not.

There is no economy tab bolted onto the side. The economy is the transmission system of the whole game. Close the Strait of Hormuz and Brent spikes; oil feeds the inflation print; the print reaches the central bank, the markets, and eventually the voters. Sanction Beijing and trade reroutes, growth bleeds on both sides of the Pacific, and the S&P answers within the week. Every shock propagates through the same chain of consequence — a war moves oil, oil moves the economy, and the electorate answers at the next election, if you allow one.

Industry is where that economy becomes power. Each nation's productive base — energy, resources, manufacturing — determines what it can actually sustain: the materiel a war burns through, the exports a rival can embargo, the leverage a tariff really carries. A blockade is not a status effect; it is missing inputs working through somebody's supply chain.

You hold the real levers: the budget, taxes, tariffs, sanctions, subsidies, the pressure you put on the central bank. The engine weighs every plausible outcome and hands you the bill. Cut rates and the market cheers today; the inflation print answers in six months.

Nothing is scripted. No two economies collapse the same way.
